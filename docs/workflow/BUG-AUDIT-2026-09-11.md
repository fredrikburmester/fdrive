## Real but deliberately not fixed: SEC-08

A share password can be brute-forced from many source addresses: the credential cap is keyed
`(client IP, share id)`, and nothing is keyed by share id alone. The obvious remedy was
rejected. A global per-share counter would let anyone holding the link submit a handful of
wrong passwords and lock the share's legitimate recipient out for the rest of the window,
anonymously and at zero cost - a cheaper attack than the one it closes, and the same reasoning
`docs/AUTH.md` already records for login. Per-share delay or backoff was rejected too, since
held-open connections are their own denial of service.

The empty-password sub-question was investigated and is not a bug: read from pinned SFTPGo
v2.7.5 source, `""` is the sentinel for "no password" on both sides, and fdrive derives
`hasPassword` from upstream's echo, so a lock icon on an open share cannot occur. Rejecting
`""` would break both "remove password" and "create without a password".

Owner-level remedies, both outside an audit fix: rate-limit `/api/v1/public/shares/*` at the
edge proxy keyed by IPv6 /64 rather than /128, and require a minimum share-password strength.

## Blocked on uncommitted work: DB-13

Three of the five reported indexes are real and worth adding - `idx.moves.rootId`,
`idx.scans.rootId`, and a composite `idx.ocr_log(status, at)` in that column order, because the
query filters on `status` and range-orders on `at`. Two are speculative: `idx.events` is
insert-and-notify only with no `SELECT` anywhere, and `imageEmbeddings.model` has very low
cardinality while its only unfiltered consumer scans regardless.

No migration was written. The repository owner holds an uncommitted `0002_foreign_key_indexes`
whose journal entry already claims index 2, so an audit migration would have to be `0003`
depending on an uncommitted predecessor - which would break the migration chain for any fresh
checkout. This needs `0002` committed first, then a `0003` adding the three indexes above.

## Upstream constraint: FS-06

SFTPGo 2.7.5 has no atomic conditional move/copy REST option. Checking a destination before
moving or copying cannot protect against another client writing between those operations.
A process-local mutex would not cover external clients, aliases or multiple API processes.
The audit preserves these operations and records the unresolved upstream requirement.
